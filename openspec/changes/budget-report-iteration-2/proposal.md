## Why

Today `Budget.total_amount` is purely derived — the sum of budget-line `amount`s, always in `local_currency` — and `BudgetLine.amount` has no currency of its own. There is nowhere to record what a donor actually committed when that commitment is in a different currency: a donor who says "here is €10,000" has no field to hold that figure, and a grantee building a local-currency budget against that promise has no live guide for whether they're tracking under or over it while they build — only a fully-summed `total_amount` at the end, by which point it's too late to course-correct incrementally. Once real spending starts, the real bank conversion rate (tracked by the separate currency-ledger feature, `FundingReceipt`/`CurrencyConversion`) will diverge from whatever rate the grantee estimated at budget-creation time — but today there's no estimate recorded to diverge *from*, and nothing marking when a budget's numbers were actually locked in. Separately, the budget-lines table (`Amount`/`Used` columns) only ever renders `local_currency`, with no way for a donor-currency-native funder to see line-level figures in their own currency, and the backend has no data to support that even if the UI wanted it.

Two more gaps surfaced while scoping this: the sidebar's "Reports" nav item links to `/reports`, a route that doesn't exist — it silently redirects to `/dashboard`, and there's no way today to see reports across more than one budget at a time. And `/dashboard` itself is entirely hardcoded mock data (`Dashboard.tsx`'s stats array, commented `// Mock stats data - replace with real data from API`) — a grantee managing several budgets across several donor currencies has no real picture of what they're committed to, what's landed, what's been converted, or how a single shared local-currency bank account splits across their donor projects (the alternative most grantees resort to today is a separate bank account per project, purely for accounting separation).

## What Changes

- Add `Budget.donor_total_amount`, a directly-entered figure denominated in `actual_currency` representing the donor's stated commitment (e.g. "€10,000"), editable only while the budget is unconfirmed (mirrors the existing `is_budget_locked` metadata-lock rule).
- Add `Budget.estimated_exchange_rate`, a directly-entered figure (e.g. `0.8`) representing the grantee's own best guess, at the moment they pick it, of the `actual_currency` → `local_currency` rate — entered up front, before or while budget lines are being built, so the grantee has an immediate local-currency guide to build against rather than only finding out where they landed after the fact. Editable pre-confirmation, locked once confirmed, persisted from first entry so it survives a grantee saving a half-built draft and returning later. This is explicitly a *planning-time estimate the grantee chooses*, distinct from the currency-ledger's real, bank-derived conversion rate (which is derived from two real amounts, never entered).
- Derive (never store) an **estimated local cap** — `donor_total_amount × estimated_exchange_rate` — exposed as a read-only computed figure wherever a budget is returned, for comparison against the real, running `total_amount` while the grantee builds.
- Add `Budget.confirmed_at`, a timestamp set when a budget transitions to `confirmed` (reset if later reverted to `draft` and re-confirmed). Anchors the audit question "as of when was this estimated rate treated as final" without adding a separate date field for the rate itself.
- Surface the donor commitment, the grantee's estimated rate, and the estimated local cap everywhere a budget total is currently shown (single-budget view, budget edit form, donor dashboard), in the pattern "€10,000 EUR @ 0.8 (est.) ≈ £8,000 GBP" — falling back to today's local-only display when no `actual_currency`/`donor_total_amount`/`estimated_exchange_rate` is set (the common case, unaffected).
- Add a **Local / Donor (estimated) / Both** display toggle to the budget-lines table, applying to both the `Amount` and `Used` columns, converting local-currency figures to their donor-currency equivalent via `estimated_exchange_rate`. The toggle only appears when a valid rate exists; converted figures are labeled as estimated, not treated as actual per-line FX facts.
- Fix the dead `/reports` nav link (it currently falls through to a catch-all redirect to `/dashboard`) by adding a **cross-budget reports directory**: one page listing every report across every budget the user owns (or, for a donor, every budget they fund), with **Budget (program)**, **Donor/Funder**, **Period**, and **Status** columns, a **View Budget** action alongside the existing **View Report**, and filters by status, budget, and donor. Backed by a new endpoint — today only a per-budget report list (`GET /reports/by-budget/{budget_id}`) exists.
- Replace the grantee-facing `Dashboard.tsx` — currently 100% hardcoded mock stats fetching nothing — with a real dashboard backed by a new aggregation endpoint, showing: budget counts by status; total **committed** per donor currency (derived from each confirmed budget's real, built `total_amount` converted back via its `estimated_exchange_rate` — not the flat `donor_total_amount` promise, since what's actually budgeted can undershoot what was promised); total **received** per donor currency (real `FundingReceipt` sums); **conversion progress** per donor currency (amount and % of received actually converted, via `CurrencyConversion`); and a **per-budget breakdown table** (converted/spent/remaining in local currency per budget) — surfacing the currency-ledger's existing per-budget FIFO tracking, which already keeps each budget's slice of a single shared local-currency bank account separately attributable (the real-world problem this solves: grantees historically needed one bank account per donor project to keep funds separately accountable; the ledger already tracks that separation internally per budget, this just makes it visible in aggregate).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `budget-donor-commitment`: backend support for a directly-entered donor-currency budget total (`donor_total_amount`), a directly-entered planning-time exchange-rate estimate (`estimated_exchange_rate`), a derived estimated local cap, and a `confirmed_at` timestamp — all editable only pre-confirmation.
- `budget-currency-display-ui`: frontend display of the donor commitment / estimated rate / estimated local cap wherever budget totals appear, plus a Local/Donor(estimated)/Both toggle on the budget-lines table's Amount and Used columns.
- `report-directory-api`: backend endpoint listing reports across all of a user's budgets (not scoped to one `budget_id`), with status/budget/donor filters and budget/funder details joined onto each report.
- `report-directory-ui`: frontend reports-directory page (fixing the dead `/reports` nav link), with the Budget/Donor columns, View Budget action, and status/budget/donor filters.
- `grantee-dashboard-api`: backend aggregation endpoint for the grantee-facing dashboard — budgets-by-status counts, committed/received/converted totals grouped by currency (never blended), and a per-budget local-currency breakdown.
- `grantee-dashboard-ui`: frontend dashboard replacing `Dashboard.tsx`'s hardcoded mock stats with the real aggregation data above.

### Modified Capabilities
(none — no existing spec covers base `Budget`/`Report` CRUD or either frontend page's display today, so nothing here changes a previously-specified requirement)

## Impact

- **New code (backend)**: migration adding `budgets.donor_total_amount`, `budgets.estimated_exchange_rate` (both nullable float), and `budgets.confirmed_at` (nullable timestamp); `BudgetCreate`/`BudgetUpdate`/`BudgetPatched`/response schemas gain the new fields plus a computed `estimated_local_cap`; `_is_metadata_edit` (`services/budget/app/services/budget_services.py`) gains the new editable fields to the pre-confirmation-only edit check; the confirm/revert transition logic in `update_budget_service` sets/resets `confirmed_at`. A new `GET /reports/` endpoint (role-scoped like the existing `/budgets/` vs `/budgets/funded/` split), with an extended report response schema (budget name/status/funder, via eager-load). A new grantee-dashboard aggregation endpoint reusing the existing per-budget `get_ledger_balance_service` for the breakdown table plus new grouped-sum queries for the three currency-grouped totals.
- **Modified code (frontend)**: `frontend-typescript/src/pages/Budgets/types/budget.ts` (new fields), the budget edit form (new fields), `BudgetViewHeader.tsx`/`SingleBudgetView.tsx` (donor-equivalent display), `DonorDashboard.tsx` (same, where budget totals list), `BudgetViewLinesTable.tsx` (currency toggle + converted Amount/Used rendering); a new reports-directory page plus its `/reports` route in `App.tsx`; `Dashboard.tsx` rewired from mock data to the new aggregation endpoint.
- **Out of scope**: a full status-transition history table (who/when/from→to for every status change, not just the latest confirmation) — this need is real and already tracked as GitHub issue #138, but is deliberately backlogged here since this is pre-launch/demo-stage work with no production history to lose yet; `confirmed_at` (a single scalar, not a log) is enough for now. Applying the estimate to `DonorDashboard`'s aggregate `total_allocated` figures (spec'd elsewhere as a straight sum of `total_amount`, grouped by currency per the mixed-currency fix — extending it to donor-currency display is a natural follow-up, not bundled here). Any per-line *actual* FX trace (that's the currency-ledger's FIFO allocation trail, already out of scope in the prior `budget-report-frontend` change, and stays out of scope here since converted line figures in this change are explicitly estimates, not real bank-traced amounts). Auto-generating budget lines from the donor total (the grantee still builds lines manually — this change only records and displays the target/estimate, it doesn't drive line entry). Reconciling the dashboard's tracked local-currency totals against a manually-entered real bank balance (a validation/alerting feature, not requested here — the dashboard surfaces what the ledger tracks and trusts it).
